# Privacy Policy for EchoType

**Last Updated: February 6, 2026**

EchoType ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use our desktop application.

## 1. Core Privacy Philosophy: Offline-First
EchoType is designed as an **offline-first** application. Our primary goal is to provide powerful AI voice-to-text capabilities while ensuring that your data stays on your local machine.

## 2. Information Collection and Processing

### 2.1 Audio Data
- **Local Processing**: By default, all voice-to-text transcription is performed **locally** on your device using built-in AI models (e.g., Paraformer or Qwen3-ASR). Your raw audio data never leaves your computer during standard offline transcription.
- **Recording Storage**: EchoType may store temporary audio buffers to facilitate transcription. These are managed locally and are not uploaded to any remote servers by EchoType.

### 2.2 Transcribed Text
- **Local History**: Your transcription history is stored locally in a database on your device. This data is not synchronized to any cloud service provided by us.
- **AI Integrations**: If you explicitly enable and use third-party integrations (such as ChatGPT, Claude, or OpenClaw), the transcribed text will be sent to those respective service providers via their APIs. Please review the privacy policies of these third-party services before using them.

### 2.3 Configuration and Settings
- All application settings, including API keys for optional integrations, are stored locally on your device. API keys are encrypted using your system's secure storage (e.g., Electron's `safeStorage`).

## 3. Third-Party Services (Optional)
EchoType allows you to connect to external AI services. When you use these integrations:
- We act as a bridge, sending only the necessary data (the text you wish to process) to the service you have configured.
- We do not intercept, store, or monitor the content of these external communications.

## 4. Analytics and Telemetry
- EchoType **does not** include any hidden tracking, analytics, or telemetry. We do not collect "usage data" or "crash reports" unless you manually provide them to us for troubleshooting.

## 5. Security
- We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your local data, including encrypting sensitive configuration information. However, the security of your local data also depends on the security of your operating system and device.

## 6. Your Rights
Since all data is stored locally, you have total control over your information:
- You can delete your transcription history at any time within the app.
- You can uninstall the application and manually delete its local data folder to remove all traces of use.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

## 8. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our official GitHub repository.

# EchoType 隐私政策

**最后更新日期：2026年2月6日**

EchoType（“我们”或“本应用程序”）致力于保护您的隐私。本隐私政策说明了您在使用我们的桌面应用程序时，我们关于信息收集、使用和披露的做法。

## 1. 核心隐私理念：离线优先
EchoType 被设计为一款**离线优先**的应用。我们的核心目标是在提供强大 AI 语音转文字功能的同时，确保您的数据保留在您的本地设备上。

## 2. 信息收集与处理

### 2.1 音频数据
- **本地处理**：在默认情况下，所有语音转文字转录均使用内置 AI 模型（如 Paraformer 或 Qwen3-ASR）在您的设备上**本地执行**。在标准的离线转录过程中，您的原始音频数据绝不会离开您的计算机。
- **录音存储**：EchoType 可能会存储临时音频缓存以协助转录。这些缓存全部在本地管理，不会被上传到我们的任何远程服务器。

### 2.2 转录文本
- **本地历史记录**：您的转录历史记录存储在您设备上的本地数据库中。这些数据不会同步到我们提供的任何云服务。
- **AI 集成**：如果您明确启用并使用第三方集成服务（如 ChatGPT、Claude 或 OpenClaw），转录的文本将通过相应服务的 API 发送给这些服务提供商。请在使用这些第三方服务前查阅其各自的隐私政策。

### 2.3 配置与设置
- 所有应用程序设置，包括可选集成的 API 密钥，均存储在您的本地设备上。敏感信息（如 API 密钥）使用系统的安全存储（如 Electron 的 `safeStorage`）进行加密。

## 3. 第三方服务（可选）
EchoType 允许您连接到外部 AI 服务。当您使用这些集成时：
- 我们仅作为中转，将必要的数据（您希望处理的文本）发送到您配置的服务中。
- 我们不会拦截、存储或监控这些外部通信的内容。

## 4. 分析与遥测
- EchoType **不包含**任何隐藏的追踪、分析或遥测代码。除非您为了排除故障而手动向我们提供，否则我们不会收集任何“使用数据”或“崩溃报告”。

## 5. 安全性
- 我们采取行业标准的管理措施来保护您的本地数据，包括对敏感配置信息的加密。然而，本地数据的安全性也取决于您的操作系统及设备的安全性。

## 6. 您的权利
由于所有数据均存储在本地，您对自己的信息拥有完全的控制权：
- 您可以随时在应用内删除转录历史记录。
- 您可以卸载应用程序并手动删除其本地数据文件夹，以清除所有使用痕迹。

## 7. 政策修订
我们可能会不时更新隐私政策。如有更改，我们将通过在本页面发布新的隐私政策并更新“最后更新日期”来通知您。

## 8. 联系我们
如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过我们的官方 GitHub 仓库联系我们。
