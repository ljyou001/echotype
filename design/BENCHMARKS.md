> Generated by the prototype's `scripts/bench_engine.py` (git history, commit c58b105 and later). Kept as the baseline the native
> engine must match. The run was stopped after SenseVoice when the engine moved to Rust; RSS is omitted because the
> script measured the venv launcher process, not the interpreter.

Machine: 13th Gen Intel(R) Core(TM) i9-13980HX (32 logical cores) · NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 Laptop GPU · Windows-10-10.0.26300-SP0

KPI: `release_to_final_ms` = engine time from `session.stop` receipt to `final` send (ENGINE_PROTOCOL §5).
Audio enters through `--fake-mic` at real-time pace and takes the production path (pump → VAD → decode).
`tail` = silence between the end of speech and the key release: 0 ms is the worst case, 300 ms is typical.
Each cell is P50 / P95 over the repetitions; `client` is the stop→final round trip seen by the WS client.
