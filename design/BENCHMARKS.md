# Engine latency benchmarks

## Native engine (Rust)

> Generated by `echotype-bench report` (engine/src/bin/echotype-bench.rs). Rerun the tool; do not edit by hand.

KPI: `release_to_final_ms` = engine time from `session.stop` receipt to `final` send (ENGINE_PROTOCOL §5).
Audio enters through `--fake-mic` at real-time pace and takes the production path (pump → VAD → decode).
`tail` = silence between the end of speech and the key release: 0 ms is the worst case, 300 ms is typical.
Cells are P50 / P95 over the repetitions; `client` is the stop→final round trip seen by the WebSocket client.
RSS is the engine process only: llama.cpp variants hold their model in a `llama-server` child process (not counted).
